## Summary

The selected studies provide evidence that Large Language Models and Generative AI can support software test case generation. In particular, Study 15 is highly relevant to the research topic because it evaluates multiple LLMs, including ChatGPT and Claude, and applies Equivalence Partitioning (EP) and Boundary Value Analysis (BVA). Studies 14 and 19 provide additional evidence about the use of LLMs or Generative AI for systematic test generation and the application of software testing techniques.

These studies will be used as supporting evidence for comparing ChatGPT and Claude in designing UAT test cases using EP-BVA on the PURE system.
