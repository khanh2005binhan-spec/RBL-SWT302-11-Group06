# Team Evidence Table: Final Selection (filtered from the 68-row merged draft)

**Research question:** For 20 frozen functional requirements from PURE, how do ChatGPT and Claude using the same one-shot EP–BVA prompt differ in coverage-item percentage relative to a human-designed test suite produced before viewing any AI output?

Values in the rows are copied from the merged draft without changes (Vietnamese cells translated into English). The selection and the role of each paper are my own assessment against each component of the RQ and are not the PL's final INCLUDE decision.

## 1. Why these papers

| # | Paper | Role for the RQ | Matches | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | R001 Sisomboon 2024 (prompt templates for UAT) | Compares ChatGPT with Claude on EP/BVA, measures coverage % | I, O, EP-BVA | 8 requirements from one company; dataset not verified from full text |
| 2 | R022 Sisomboon 2026 (ensemble LLM + RAG) | Claude and ChatGPT on EP/BVA/Use Case/State Transition against a gold standard | I, O, EP-BVA, reference baseline | Self-built benchmark; EP/BVA are 100% for all models (ceiling risk); table numbers missing |
| 3 | CASCON 2025 (UAT generation) | Uses PURE and compares with human-written tests | P (PURE), human baseline | Open-source LLMs only; BERTScore instead of coverage-item |
| 4 | R010 LLM4Fin (ISSTA 2024) | Evidence on humans vs ChatGPT in coverage | Human baseline, O | Financial domain; no EP/BVA, no Claude |
| 5 | ICIIBMS 2024 (on-premises) | Basis for a one-shot prompt (73.9% vs zero-shot 67.7%) | I (prompting), O | Llama models; coverage judged manually |

No single paper combines PURE, ChatGPT and Claude, EP-BVA, a human baseline made before viewing AI output, and coverage-item. That is the research gap for the team.
